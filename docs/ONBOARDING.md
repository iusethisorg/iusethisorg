# SOP: Joining the governance program

How a contributor becomes a title-holder. The path is deliberately short
— merit should be visible within weeks, not years — and every step is
positive reinforcement: each stage is a recognition, not a gate for its
own sake.

## Prerequisites

One merged contribution of any kind: a record, a write-up, a proposal
that passed, or substantive review on someone else's. (This is the
do-ocracy filter: governance is held by people who do the work.)

## The procedure

1. **Apply.** Open an issue with the *Join governance* template: which
   title you seek, which object you'd profess (your credential is your
   use), and links to your merged work.
2. **Sponsorship.** Any current title-holder may sponsor by commenting
   `sponsoring` with one specific sentence about the applicant's work.
   Sponsorship is itself recognition; unsponsored applications stay open
   30 days, then close without prejudice — apply again anytime.
3. **Acceptance.** Lazy consensus (72h) among current holders of the
   sought title, plus Steward assent during Phase 0. Objections must cite
   work, never vibes.
4. **Seating.** A PR adds the new holder to `docs/TITLES.md` and the
   matching GitHub team on `iusethis/iusethis`. Merging that PR *is* the
   appointment — no ceremony beyond the merge commit.
5. **First act.** The new holder picks their own first task from the
   backlog (nobody assigns it), and is introduced in the next Herald
   announcement.

## Holding a title

- Hold as many titles as you genuinely work; delegate freely
  (PR to `TITLES.md`; delegates act with your authority, you remain
  answerable, you may revoke by PR).
- Titles lapse after 6 months of inactivity — silently, without shame,
  reversible by the same procedure above.
- Do the work in the open; log decisions in `docs/decisions/`; give
  citations generously.

## Leaving

Resign any title by PR. A resignation merges without vote. Thank-yous go
in the PR thread; the citation ledger keeps what you built.
