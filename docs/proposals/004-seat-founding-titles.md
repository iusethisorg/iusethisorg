## Notes

Seating all titles in one person is Phase 0 working as designed, not a
loophole: the registry makes the concentration *visible*, the veto log
makes it *accountable*, and the delegation mechanism makes it
*temporary*. The first onboarded contributor should expect an offered
delegation within their first month.
